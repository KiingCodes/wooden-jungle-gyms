# Wooden Jungle Gyms

Build a Pixel-Accurate Wooden Jungle Gyms Website



Create a beautiful, fully responsive, professional website for a wooden jungle gym company called WoodLand Jungle Gyms.



IMPORTANT: Use the uploaded reference screenshot as the primary design blueprint. Recreate its layout, proportions, colours, typography, spacing, imagery placement and overall appearance as closely as possible. Do not redesign it, simplify it, or substitute a different template.



1. Overall Design Direction



The website must have a premium, welcoming, nature-inspired aesthetic.



Design characteristics:



- White and warm cream backgrounds.

- Forest green as the primary brand colour.

- Natural wooden brown and light green accents.

- Modern, friendly typography.

- Rounded buttons and subtle shadows.

- Clean spacing and balanced sections.

- High-quality, realistic photography.

- Professional desktop layout with excellent mobile responsiveness.



Avoid excessive gradients, oversized decorative elements, poster-style layouts, and unnecessary animations.



2. Header / Navigation



Create a slim white navigation bar.



Left:



- WoodLand logo featuring a tree and wooden jungle gym symbol.

- Brand name: WoodLand.

- Small subtitle: JUNGLE GYMS.



Centre navigation:



- Home

- Our Jungle Gyms

- Gallery

- About Us

- Contact



Right:



- Search icon.

- Shopping cart icon.

- Dark green rounded "Get a Quote" button.



The header should remain clean and compact.



3. Hero Section



Create a large, full-width hero section directly below the navigation.



Use a realistic photograph of a beautiful wooden jungle gym installed in a green backyard, with children enjoying the equipment.



The photograph should dominate the right side while the left side has a subtle dark overlay for readable text.



Text on the left:



Small uppercase label:

WOODEN JUNGLE GYMS



Main heading:

"Real Play.

Lasting Memories."



Make "Real Play." white and "Lasting Memories." light green.



Description:

"Beautifully crafted wooden jungle gyms, designed to inspire active play, build confidence and create unforgettable childhood moments."



Add a light-green rounded button:

"Explore Our Jungle Gyms →"



The hero should be approximately 390–420px tall on desktop.



4. Features Section



Create a horizontal four-column feature strip on a warm off-white background.



Each feature should have a simple dark-green outline icon, bold heading and short description.



Feature 1:

Premium Quality Wood

Durable, safe and built to last for years.



Feature 2:

Child Safety First

Designed with safety in mind, always.



Feature 3:

Custom Designs

Tailored to your space, your style, your kids.



Feature 4:

Nationwide Delivery

We bring the adventure to your doorstep.



Use subtle vertical dividers between features.



5. About Section



Create a three-column horizontal section.



Left:

A large rectangular photograph showing a happy child playing inside a wooden jungle gym.



Centre:

Small uppercase heading:

MORE THAN JUST A PLAYGROUND



Main heading:

"It's a place for growth, adventure and joy."



Description:

"Our wooden jungle gyms are more than just play equipment — they're a space where kids can climb, explore, imagine and grow."



Add a dark-green button:

"About Our Story →"



Right:

A soft pale-green textured background panel containing four benefits with small outline icons:



- Encourages active play and healthy living.

- Builds strength, balance and coordination.

- Sparks creativity and imagination.

- Brings families together outdoors.



6. Product Collection Section



Add a centred section heading:



"Our Jungle Gyms Collection"



Include a short subtitle:

"From smaller backyard setups to large adventure parks, we have the perfect fit for your space and budget."



Display four equally sized product image cards in one horizontal row.



Card 1: Classic Playsets

Card 2: Adventure Towers

Card 3: Custom Builds

Card 4: Accessories



Each card must:



- Have a large, realistic product photograph.

- Use slightly rounded corners.

- Have a dark gradient overlay at the bottom.

- Display its category name in white.

- Include a small circular arrow icon.



Make the cards clickable and link them to their respective product categories.



7. Footer



Create a full-width dark forest-green footer strip.



Left:

Small tree logo.



Centre:

"Play Today. Stronger Tomorrow."



Add three small feature highlights:



- Safe & Durable

- Eco-Friendly Materials

- Nationwide Delivery



Right:

A white outlined rounded "Get a Quote" button.



8. Functionality



This must be a real working website, not a static visual mockup.



Implement:



- Functional navigation links.

- Responsive mobile navigation.

- Working product category links.

- Interactive gallery.

- Working quote enquiry form.

- WhatsApp enquiry button.

- Clickable contact information.

- Smooth scrolling.

- Hover effects on buttons and product cards.



The Get a Quote form should collect:



- Customer name.

- Phone number.

- Email address.

- Preferred jungle gym.

- Location.

- Additional requirements.



Do not invent real company contact details or product prices. Use clearly identifiable placeholders where information is unavailable.



9. Technical Requirements



- Use React and Tailwind CSS.

- Write clean, reusable components.

- Use Lucide icons.

- Ensure the website works on desktop, tablet and mobile.

- Optimise all images for fast loading.

- Use semantic HTML and accessible buttons.

- Maintain consistent spacing, typography and colours throughout.



10. Final Design Instruction



The uploaded screenshot is the source of truth.



Prioritise visual accuracy over creative reinterpretation.



Pay particular attention to:



- The exact section order.

- Header proportions.

- Hero image positioning.

- Text alignment.

- Four-column feature strip.

- Three-column about section.

- Four product cards.

- Compact green footer.



Do not turn the website into a landing-page poster or a single large promotional banner.



I want an authentic, professionally developed business website that looks like the uploaded reference when viewed in a browser.



Build the complete homepage, not just a wireframe.
